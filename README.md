# Simple_micro-bit_Calculator
## Description
### Materials
You need...
+ a micro:bit with battery pack 🖥️ 🔋
+ the [hex file for the micro:bit](https://downloads.scratch.mit.edu/microbit/scratch-microbit.hex.zip) 📁
+ a little bit of aluminum foil or your hands 🙌
+ [ScratchLink](https://scratch.mit.edu/download/scratch-link) and [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/1388747806/) or [Xcratch](https://xcratch.github.io/editor/#1792240321911) 🌐
### Instructions
<ol type="1">
  <li>Put the file for Scratch on the micro:bit</li>
  <li>Open <a href="https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/1388747806/">the project</a></li>
  <li>Connect the micro:bit to Scratch
    <ul>
      <li>Open the Editor</li>
      <li>Go to the micro:bit extension</li>
      <li>Tap on the <strong>!</strong> and choose your micro:bit.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Then connect one end of the aluminum foil to the GND pin or touch it with one finger</li>
  <li>With the other side of your aluminum foil (or your finger), touch the 0, 1 or 2 pin to choose Number 1, 2 and the operator</li>
  <li>Press <strong>A</strong> to see the Equal</li>
</ol>
